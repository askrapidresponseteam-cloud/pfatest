PFA Sirohi

7 DAY DONKEY WELFARE AND TREATMENT CAMP

Wounded, suffering donkeys will get protection and medicine. In the rural area of Sirohi district, Free camp under the leadership of PFA Chairperson Smt. MANEKA SANJAY GANDHI,PFA SIROHI has organised 7 days donkey and mule treatment camp. 9 Donkeys and mule were adopted by PFA SHELTER and 87 donkeys were treated on the spot.

PROVIDED RELIEF TO LIFE STOCK BY PROVIDING FODDER AND WATER IN BARMER REGION ACHIEVEMENT.

Thousands of animals were affected due to famine like situation arised due to less rain in districts of Sirohi , Pali , Jaisalmer and most importantly BARMER region of Jodhpur division of Rajasthan state. Due to less rain there was not enough fodder, animals were losing there precious life. In this condition PFA Sirohi chose Barmer district for providing cattle feed purchased at very high cost from M.P, Gujarat and Haryana and provided relief to the animals. In this life saving work, SH. JAYESH BHAI SHAH trustee of Youth charitable trust VALKESHWAR , Mumbai and his team provided economic support of around 22 Lakhs . The work done by PFA SIROHI in this situation was commendable. The work was also appreciated by District Administration Barmer. Important NEWSPAPERS also highlighted this work done by PFA SIROHI in their important articles. It is an another milestone for PFA SIROHI.

PFA Chairperson Mrs. MANEKA SANJAY GANDHI is requested to provide Letter Of Compliment to Jayesh Bhai , Trusty of Youth Charitable Trust and Roshan bhai Trusty of Shree Surat Panjrapol Jivdaya so that these youths are motivated. Please send the letter of compliment to address below.

Wildlife Relief to Drinking Water Facility ( Drinking Water Facility For Wild Animals In Forest Area Through 23 Water Tankers Operated By PFA Till 30 June 2019 )

In the summer season of drought year , for quenching the thrist of live stock in forest land of Sirohi district as well as public places daily 23 water tankers were supplied from 1 APRIL to 30 MAY and daily 23 water tankers were supplied . in this live saving job local Bhamashah and migrants helped a lot . In this work many Bamashah supplied from 1 tanker to 30 tankers for drinking purpose. Economical help of 3 LAKHS 72 THOUSAND were donated and this life saving job was executed under PFA SIROHI.
