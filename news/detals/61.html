PFA Ghaziabad

PFA Ghaziabad Last night on the basis on an Information PFA caught a Butchers Truck (10 Tyra Large Body Truck) at Vijay Nagar Ghaziabad and rescued 35 buffaloes in a very dehydrated condition, all were going for slaughter in a illegal slaughter house in UP ... 3 butchers arrested and a case has been registered under section 380(Theft Non bailable) 429,506, and PCA Act in PS Vijay Nagar Ghaziabad.
