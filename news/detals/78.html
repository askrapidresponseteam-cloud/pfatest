PFA Pirli (Chandrapur)

PFA Pirli (Chandrapur) Report of 19 Animals 16 oxes , 3 Cows,were handed over, by Mulchera police Dist. Gadchiroli to PFA shelter house Pirli. The animals were rescued by Mulchera police from a unknown person after a tip given by one local person. The exact spot where the animals were rescued is around 162 kms from Chandrapur
