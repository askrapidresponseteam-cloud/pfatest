DO YOU FEED YOUR COMMUNITY ANIMALS?

We at People For Animals care about regular feeders. We are happy to assist you through our Feeder Care Assistance Program.

If you are a student, housemaker, or unable to manage your regular feeding expenses then enroll in our PFA Feeder Care Assistance.

In response to the necessity of feeding starving stray animals, we have started supporting regular feeders in Delhi-NCR and Mumbai with food for their daily food drives.

In order to support individual feeders, who are unable to manage their feeding drives due to a shortage of finances or lack of support, we have been raising and distributing food. We are now establishing a feeder care assistance program in which in-kind donations are provided to feeders across the Delhi-NCR and Mumbai.

If you are someone/know someone (who is not on social media), who takes care of community animals and needs help with the food then kindly fill out the form below.

Note: We will not be able to provide every entry with the food but will try to shortlist a few.

Objective & Strategy

  • 1
    Create Infrastructure

    Upgrade and establish more facilities for animal rescue and rehabilitation such as shelters, ambulances, mobile clinics, and helplines.

  • 2
    Influence Policy

    Act as a pressure group to bring about environmental and animal-friendly legislation.

  • 3
    Ensure Implementation

    Take direct action through PFA Units all over India to protect animals and punish offenders.

  • 4
    Educate and Sensitize
    1. Create Animal Welfare Groups In Schools and Colleges
    2. Spread awareness on social media platforms to sensitize people on animal-related issues and cruelties.
    3. Conduct training programs for lawyers, police officers, Judiciary and Bureaucrat and animal owners.
    4. Publish and produce books and expert guides on animal care and related issues.
    5. Partner with influential community leaders and opinion-makers to spread the animal movement globally.
  • 5
    Advance the Movement
    1. Liaise with national and international animal welfare groups.
    2. Provide support, financial or otherwise, to deserving animal welfare initiatives.
    3. Provide career opportunities in animal welfare.
  • 6
    Ensure Implementation
    1. Educate the citizens in avenues like health, environmental, and economic costs of animal-based choices and sensitize them to shift to a plant based lifestyle.
    2. Source and promote animal-free alternatives.
    3. Enrol celebrity spokespersons to popularize cruelty-free choices.

Animal welfare is not just about animals. It is about us. Our living conditions, our children, our earth. Cruelty to animals has a significant and irreversible impact on peaceful societies, human health, the economy, and the environment.
