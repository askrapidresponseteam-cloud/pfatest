When a cow grows old and stops producing milk, its owner abandons them as they’re no longer useful to them. Thousands of helpless cows are abandoned on the streets. Many of these are just elderly, injured, or abandoned. Most of the time butchers take advantage of these cows. Others are very malnourished and roam from place to place in quest of food, facing the brunt of the harsh weather. Some of these have deadly accidents, while others howl in agony as they wait for medical assistance owing to serious injuries. These cows' lives have become horrible.

Should this be the end of cows that have provided us with milk for generations?

Come forward now and do something for them! Only in Rs. 100 you can give them a better life.

Animal welfare is not just about animals. It is about us. Our living conditions, our children, our earth. Cruelty to animals has a significant and irreversible impact on peaceful societies, human health, the economy, and the environment.
