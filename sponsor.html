Why Sponsor?

Pick an animal of your choice and contribute towards its monthly upkeep. You can visit our hospital/shelter and choose your pet, or leave that to us. We will give you the name and details of your sponsored pet and send you constant updates. All sponsors are welcome to visit their sponsored pets and spend time with them. Whenever you can, we encourage you to visit your animal pal.
